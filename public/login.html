Tallyhouse

See where your money actually goes.

  • Every account in one place. Banks and brokerages connect through Lunchflow. Foreign payments are converted at the rate on the day, refunds are matched to the purchase they reverse, and transfers between your own accounts cancel out.
  • Categories you control. Plain keyword rules, not a black box — and an AI review that proposes them, flags amounts that look unusual for a payee, and points out merchants widely reported as scams.
  • Nobody else's business. No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Access is read-only, so it can never move money, and you can export or delete everything at any time.
