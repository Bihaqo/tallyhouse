What it is

A dashboard that reads your bank and brokerage accounts through Lunchflow and sorts your spending into categories, using keyword rules you control and an AI review. It only ever reads your accounts; it cannot move money.

The numbers are not advice, and not guaranteed

Categories come from keyword matching and a language model, and both get things wrong. A miscategorised transaction moves real money between totals. Foreign amounts are converted at historical reference rates, and refunds are matched to purchases by a heuristic that can mispair them.

Treat everything here as a convenient view, not a source of truth. It is not financial, tax or accounting advice. Check anything that matters against your bank or your accountant before relying on it.

Costs are yours

Tallyhouse itself is free. It runs on API keys you supply, and those are billed to you by the companies that issue them. Lunchflow charges its own subscription.

The AI review is optional: it only runs if you put an OpenAI key on your account, and setup does not require one. If you do, it is billed by OpenAI to that key automatically — finishing setup starts a pass over however many months you chose there, and new transactions are reviewed as they arrive. Those charges are your responsibility. Settings → AI review spend shows a running total, and Settings → API keys lets you remove the key at any time, which stops it.
