Lunchflow connects your banks and brokerages — thousands of institutions across 30+ countries — and serves them over one API. This dashboard reads your accounts and transactions from it; it never touches your bank directly, and it only ever reads.

This dashboard is completely free. For Lunchflow, the first 7 days are free with no card required; after that it is about $2.92 per month billed annually ($34.99 a year), covering two connected accounts, with extra connections at $10 each. See lunchflow.app/pricing for the current figure.

Getting a key

  1. Register at lunchflow.app and start the free trial.
  2. Connect your bank or brokerage from the Lunchflow dashboard.
  3. Still in the dashboard, add an API destination.
  4. Open that destination's settings and copy its API key.
  5. Paste it above.

What the AI review is for

Categories are matched by plain keyword filters. The review is what fills those in, and what keeps an eye on what they catch:

  • Flags potentially suspicious transactions — an amount far outside what a payee usually charges, a likely duplicate charge, or a merchant widely reported as a scam.
  • Suggests filters — keyword rules that assign uncategorised transactions to a category, each one shown with exactly what it would catch before you accept it.

Powers the AI review of transactions, billed to your own OpenAI account. Leave it blank to set up without the review — everything else works, and you can switch it on later in Settings.

The AI review reads one transaction at a time to suggest a category, propose a keyword rule, and flag amounts that look unusual for that merchant. Without a key none of that runs: your categories are matched by the keyword rules alone, which you edit in Settings.

There is no subscription — OpenAI bills your own account for what you use. Reviews are cached, so each transaction is paid for once; after the first pass only new transactions cost anything. Settings → AI usage keeps a running total.

Getting a key

  1. Register at platform.openai.com.
  2. Under Billing, add a payment method and some credit — a new account's key fails until it has credit.
  3. Go to API keys → Create new secret key.
  4. Copy it straight away — OpenAI shows the key only once — and paste it above.

Categories for your spending

Want them suggested for you? Tallyhouse can send your payee names — up to 300 of them, most frequent first — to OpenAI once, and propose a category list from what it sees. Names only: no amounts, no dates, no account details, and nothing about you.

This one call runs on Tallyhouse's own OpenAI key, so it costs you nothing and needs no OpenAI account. The request is not stored in that account, nothing from it is kept here, and it is offered once per account.
